# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Reveric is for long-form fiction writers who want AI help without giving up authorship. They work in extended sessions, often moving between outline, character notes, worldbuilding, chapter drafting, revision, and AI-assisted polishing.

Primary users need a calm workspace that keeps story assets connected. They are not trying to chat with a generic assistant; they are trying to turn structured creative intent into coherent chapters while preserving voice, continuity, and control.

## Product Purpose

Reveric is an AI-assisted novel writing application. It combines chapter planning, AI expansion, manual revision, final polishing, and structured story asset management in one writing workspace.

Success means authors can build a durable creative system around a novel: world rules, characters, outlines, foreshadowing, materials, maps, agents, and chapters all remain available as context for both human decisions and AI generation.

The product should make AI feel like a disciplined creative collaborator: helpful, contextual, configurable, and always subordinate to the writer's judgment.

## Brand Personality

Calm, literary, deliberate.

Reveric should feel like a writer's study with careful tooling: warm, focused, readable, and quietly capable. The tone is practical and craft-oriented, not promotional or hyperbolic.

## Anti-references

Reveric should not look like a generic SaaS dashboard, a neon AI chat toy, a terminal-native developer tool, or a marketing landing page inside the app.

Avoid purple-blue gradient AI branding, glassmorphism, oversized hero panels, decorative metrics, noisy card grids, novelty controls, and UI chrome that competes with the manuscript.

Avoid treating AI as the protagonist. The writer, the manuscript, and the story structure are the center of the interface.

## Design Principles

1. Put the manuscript first. Editing and reading surfaces should preserve attention, line length, and rhythm before exposing secondary controls.
2. Keep story assets connected. Characters, worldbuilding, outlines, maps, agents, and chapters should feel like one workspace, not separate utilities.
3. Make AI configurable but quiet. AI providers, agents, prompts, and tools need clear controls, but their UI should stay utilitarian and restrained.
4. Prefer earned familiarity. Standard product patterns are appropriate when they reduce friction for repeated writing and management tasks.
5. Use literary warmth with discipline. Parchment tones, Georgia text, and cartographic details should support focus, not become decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Reveric should target practical WCAG AA contrast for text, controls, focus states, and semantic feedback. The app should support keyboard use for editor and management workflows, clear disabled states, reduced-motion-safe interactions, and non-color-only feedback for validation, deletion, warning, and success states.

Chinese and English localization are first-class requirements. User-visible copy should use the existing i18n system rather than hardcoded strings.
