# Single-season workflows use an implicit Current Season

Season is always persisted, but a Screenplay with one active Season hides Season controls and treats that sole Season as the implicit Current Season. Ordinary AI paths stay flat: Episode listing and creation in the current Work may omit `season_id`, while Beat and Scene operations use their Episode or stable resource ID. One AI turn freezes the editor's Current Season as its default scope; Season management and explicit cross-Season operations identify a Season directly. Current Season persistence and invalid-selection repair are defined by ADR-0030, and reference-Work scoping by ADR-0035. This preserves a low-cost single-season workflow without creating a second seasonless data model or forcing Season terminology into ordinary user conversations.
