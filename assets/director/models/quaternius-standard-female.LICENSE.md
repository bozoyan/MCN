# Quaternius Standard female actor model

Source: [Universal Base Characters — Standard (free)](https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters), `Base Characters/Godot - UE/Superhero_Female_FullBody.gltf` and `.bin`.

Creator: Quaternius. The Standard package includes `License_Standard.txt`, which dedicates the models to [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Commercial use, modification, and redistribution are permitted.

The mesh, skin, and 65-joint skeleton were taken from the converted copy in BeefTV (source images and materials dropped; the director applies its own material). The local adaptation reversed the triangle winding with `scripts/reverse_glb_winding.py` to match the director's right-handed projection. The paid Source package is not included.
