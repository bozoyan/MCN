# Example page structures

Some structures that have worked for different kinds of pages, to borrow from, cut up or ignore.
Each line: scene | what it says | a treatment that could carry it | mode. The treatments point to
recipes in the other files; any other treatment is just as valid.

## A tool or app, explained by use

1. What it does, with the product on screen | T1f (title beside the product) | ordinary
2. The main task, done once | V1 clip scrubbed by scroll, or a demo built from parts (L01) | zone
3. Three things it also does | L27 bento grid | ordinary
4. Who uses it | L21 quote, or L20 logos (real ones) | ordinary
5. Start | L24 | ordinary

## A tool or app, told as one continuous demo

1. A single held scene the length of the page: the product assembles or the task plays out as
   the reader scrolls (03 + subscribe, or a scrubbed video), captions changing beside it (08) | zone
2. Start | L24 | ordinary

## A photographer, studio or portfolio

1. The name, small, over the strongest picture | T1e, L05 | ordinary
2. The work, one picture per screen, captions with place and year | L13 with `focus(band)`, or
   L28 sticky caption | ordinary
3. A selected series as one set | I2 duotone, or a film strip (V2) | zone
4. Contact | L24 | ordinary

## An event or launch

1. The date as the headline | T1d number first, poster style | ordinary
2. What happens | L10 timeline of the day, or L04 row of sessions | zone / track
3. Who speaks | L04 row of portraits | track
4. Where | L05 + I4 slow push | ordinary
5. Tickets, questions | L22 / L23 | ordinary

## A café, shop or restaurant

1. One photo and the name | T1e, soft lifestyle style | ordinary
2. What is on the menu | L29 cut-outs over colour blocks, or L08 three cards | ordinary
3. The place | L31 essay with full-width photos | ordinary
4. Opening hours, map, booking | L24 | ordinary

## A cause, report or brand story

1. One line that states it | T1g words arriving, or T1c one huge word | ordinary
2. The facts, with real numbers | L09 + T6 count | ordinary
3. The story | L31 essay, or L28 sticky caption over archival photos (duotone mono) | ordinary
4. Then and now | L15 before and after | ordinary
5. What to do | L24 | ordinary

## A phone-first experience

The whole page as a drag sequence (`drag.md` G1): five to seven full-screen scenes, one idea
each, a picture and one line; the last scene holds the action.

## Notes

- Fewer scenes than these examples is often better; a scene without real material to fill it
  tends to become decoration.
- These are as useful for what they leave out as for what they include.
